# Job Description Scoring Rubric

## Salary (FTE AUD) — 1-10

- 10: 120K+
- 8-9: 110-120K (ideal)
- 6-7: 100-110K
- 4-5: 90-100K
- 2-3: 80-90K
- 1: <80K
- Unknown: ?/10

## Job Type — 1-10

- 10: Full-time permanent (FTE)
- 8-9: Full-time contract
- 6-7: Part-time permanent
- 4-5: Part-time contract
- 2-3: Casual / short-term
- 1: Unpaid / internship
- Unknown: ?/10

## Company Size — 1-10

- 10: Small (<50)
- 9: Medium (<200)
- 7-8: 200-500
- 5-6: Large (500-5,000)
- 3-4: Very large (5,000+)
- 1-2: Mega-corp / enterprise group / government
- Modifier: score lower on signals of bureaucracy (government, university)
- Unknown: ?/10

## Culture — 1-10

- 10: Flat / horizontal, high autonomy, fast decisions
- 8-9: Low bureaucracy, minimal structure
- 6-7: Moderate bureaucracy
- 4-5: Hierarchical, middle management
- 2-3: High bureaucracy, politics
- 1: Extreme bureaucracy / toxic politics
- Unknown: ?/10

## Location — 1-10

- 10: NSW, hybrid
- 9: NSW, remote
- 8: Remote (any state/country)
- 6-7: NSW, onsite
- 4-5: Other state, hybrid/remote
- 2-3: Other state, onsite
- 1: Requires relocation / overseas onsite
- Unknown: ?/10

## Roles — 1-10

- 10: Hybrid (AI + testing) — primary target
- 8-9: Test automation / mixed QA automation
- 6-7: Dedicated Applied AI engineer
- 4-5: Adjacent (SDET, developer w/ testing)
- 2-3: Loosely related
- 1: Unrelated
- Unknown: ?/10

## Tech Stack Match — 1-10

- 10: Exact match — all core tech aligns with your skills
- 8-9: Strong match — most core tech, minor gaps
- 6-7: Partial match — some core tech, some learning required
- 4-5: Weak match — major gaps in core tech
- 2-3: Mostly mismatched
- 1: No match — completely unfamiliar stack
- Modifier: score higher when gaps are learning opportunities (tech you want to learn); score lower when gaps are tech you don't want
- Unknown: ?/10

## AI Involvement — 1-10

- 10: Core AI — building tools, agents, workflows, enablement
- 8-9: Significant AI — AI-powered testing, automation, prompt engineering
- 6-7: Some AI — using AI tools, experimenting with use cases
- 4-5: Limited AI — occasional AI use mentioned
- 2-3: Minimal AI — AI mentioned but not core
- 1: No AI involvement
- Realism modifier: −2 to −3 when JD requires AI experience beyond your credibility (e.g. "5+ years building AI systems", "ML engineer", "production AI/ML at scale"); cap at 1-2 for extreme mismatches (e.g. "Senior ML Engineer, 10+ years")
- Unknown: ?/10

## Seniority/Level — 1-10

- 10: Mid-level — comfortable fit for experience + confidence
- 8-9: Senior at small company/team with autonomy
- 6-7: Senior at larger company — stretch, hesitation
- 4-5: Junior/entry-level — below your 7 years
- 2-3: Principal/lead/director — too senior, confidence gap
- 1: Intern/graduate — complete mismatch
- Modifier: senior roles +1-2 when Company Size score ≥ 9 AND Culture score ≥ 8; −1-2 when Company Size ≤ 5 OR Culture ≤ 5
- Unknown: ?/10

## Team — 1-10

- 10: Small team (2–5), high autonomy, AI growth
- 8-9: Small–medium (5–10), collaborative, some AI growth
- 6-7: Medium (10–20), moderate autonomy
- 4-5: Large (20+), less individual impact
- 2-3: Very large / siloed
- 1: Isolated / no growth
- Unknown: ?/10

## Onsite Days — 1-10

- 10: Hybrid, 1–2 days onsite (ideal)
- 8-9: Fully remote (0 days)
- 6-7: Hybrid, 3 days onsite
- 4-5: Hybrid, 4 days onsite
- 2-3: Fully onsite, 5 days
- 1: Fully onsite + relocation/long commute
- Unknown: ?/10

## Overall — 1-10

- Simple average of all 11 categories (salary, job type, company size, culture, location, roles, tech stack match, AI involvement, seniority/level, team, onsite days)
- Format: (average/10)-(count of unknowns), e.g. (6/10)-(3?)
- Unknowns are OK — we don't always have all details upfront and can potentially obtain them later as we learn more
