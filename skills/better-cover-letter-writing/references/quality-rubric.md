# Quality rubric

Score how well a rule was applied, from the letter's current text,
after fixing. One score per pattern row. The rule file names this
rubric and states the minimum level to close its row.

| Level | What it means |
|-------|---------------|
| poor | The pattern is still there, or the fix broke the meaning |
| acceptable | Every occurrence found was fixed or justified. The fix is plain and accurate, though one or two rewrites read awkwardly |
| good | Every occurrence found was fixed or justified, and every rewrite reads like the author's natural voice |
| excellent | Good, plus the rewrite also removed adjacent slop the rule implies, and the recorded evidence makes the change easy to re-check |

Scoring notes:

- Score the application of this one rule, not the letter overall.
- A bare minimum close is acceptable. Do not inflate; the checker
  refuses a score below the rule's minimum.
- If you cannot honestly reach the minimum, say so in the evidence
  and leave the row open. An honest open row beats a false close.
