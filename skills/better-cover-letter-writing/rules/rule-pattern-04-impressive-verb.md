# Pattern 4: Impressive verb → accurate verb

Read this rule, fix the letter, score the applied level, record
evidence. One rule at a time.

## Rule

Choose the strongest verb the evidence supports, no stronger.

## Before → After

Before:
"Drove the transformation toward automation."

After:
"Helped move the team toward automation."

Before:
"Championed a new testing strategy."

After:
"Worked with the team on a new testing strategy."

## Rubric

Quality: references/quality-rubric.md, minimum acceptable.
Coverage: references/completeness-rubric.md, minimum all.

## Coverage

The checker recounts these phrases in the letter:
"spearheaded", "orchestrated", "championed",
"drove the transformation". Coverage all requires zero remaining.

## Ask

- What was this person's actual part in this?

## Do not

- Do not turn participation into ownership.
