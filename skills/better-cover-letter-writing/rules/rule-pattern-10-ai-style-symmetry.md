# Pattern 10: AI-style symmetry → natural rhythm

Read this rule, fix the letter, score the applied level, record
evidence. One rule at a time.

## Rule

Let sentence structure follow what actually happened. A forced third
part is invented rhythm.

## Before → After

Before:
"I researched the problem, evaluated the options, and implemented the
solution."

After:
"I looked into the problem first, then tested a few approaches before
implementing the one that worked."

## Rubric

Quality: references/quality-rubric.md, minimum acceptable.
Coverage: references/completeness-rubric.md, minimum all.

## Coverage

Sweep for sentences with three parallel parts. Keep the ones that
reflect what actually happened; rewrite the ones where the rhythm
demanded a third.

## Ask

- Did three things actually happen, or did the rhythm demand a third?

## Do not

- Do not force every sentence into a neat three-part structure.
