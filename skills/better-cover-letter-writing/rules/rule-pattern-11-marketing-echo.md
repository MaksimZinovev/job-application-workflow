# Pattern 11: Marketing echo → cut it

Read this rule, fix the letter, score the applied level, record
evidence. One rule at a time.

## Rule

Restating the company's own pitch (mission, awards, culture) as
motivation. If their marketing team could write the sentence, cut it.
Motivation = specifics tied to your own work.

## Before → After

Before:
"I am inspired by your mission to redefine quality engineering and
drawn to your award-winning, innovation-first culture."

After:
"I test the exact kind of platform you run: devices reporting through
a cloud ingestion layer into customer-facing applications."

The before sentence could appear in anyone's letter to any company.
The after sentence names the applicant's own work.

## Rubric

Quality: references/quality-rubric.md, minimum acceptable.
Coverage: references/completeness-rubric.md, minimum all.

## Coverage

Sweep for sentences their marketing team could write themselves:
mission, awards, culture restated as motivation. Cut each one or tie
the motivation to your own work.

## Ask

- Could their marketing team have written this sentence?

## Do not

- Do not keep company facts as motivation unless they connect to your
  own work.
