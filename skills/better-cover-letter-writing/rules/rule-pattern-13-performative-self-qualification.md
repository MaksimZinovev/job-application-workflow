# Pattern 13: Performative self-qualification → plain fact

Read this rule, fix the letter, score the applied level, record evidence. One rule at a time.

## Rule

"Comfortable with", "happy to", "at ease with" announce an internal state to tick a box, mirroring the job ad's arrangement back as a personal trait. If the arrangement is standard for the role, state the fact and stop. "I am comfortable with hybrid work" becomes "I am based in Sydney".

## Before → After

Before:
"I am comfortable with hybrid work."

After:
"I am based in Sydney."

## Rubric

Quality: references/quality-rubric.md, minimum acceptable.
Coverage: references/completeness-rubric.md, minimum all.

## Coverage

Sweep for announced internal states (comfortable with, happy to, at ease with, excited to). State the fact and stop.

The checker recounts these phrases in the letter:
"comfortable with", "happy to", "at ease with", "excited to". Coverage all requires zero remaining.

## Ask

- What is the fact behind the announced feeling?

## Do not

- Do not mirror the job ad's arrangement back as a personal trait.
