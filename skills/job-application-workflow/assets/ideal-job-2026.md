# Ideal Job 2026

I want to capture some of the attributes of the ideal job. Why? Because at this point I obviously want to find the job as soon as possible. But based on my previous experience, I can see that next job can be better than previous one. So I want to find the job that I would enjoy that would have a good pay and help me develop my professional skills.

- salary (full-time equivalent): 110-120K AUD+ before taxes
- full-time, part-time, contract
- company size: Medium to small. I would avoid large companies, parts of the large enterprise groups, companies with bureaucracy and a lot of politics.
- culture: Lower bureaucracy. Good: horizontal (or flat) structures have few or no layers, promoting greater employee autonomy and faster decision-making. Bad: well-defined leadership at the top of the entire organization whose influence filters down to middle management and department heads. These middle managers then assign work to employees within their departments.
- location: preferably NSW, hybrid (work from home + office days), acceptable any other states, countries when remote
- roles :
  - test automation engineer
  - quality engineer
  - **Applied AI Engineer (AI)**
  - **AI Enablement Engineer**
