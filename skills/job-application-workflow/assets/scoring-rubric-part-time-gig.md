# Part-Time / Gig Scoring Rubric

Platform gigs (DataAnnotation-style) and salaried part-time roles. Side work serves the 2026 search (bridge income first, skills warm, foot in the door) and never outranks it. Categories: 1-10 bands; Unknown = ?/10. Format mirrors [[notes/2026-applications/scoring-rubric-full-time]].

## Hard gates (any fail = stop)

- Effective rate below $40 AUD/hr after platform fees (platform gigs only)
- Upfront fees, paid training, or equipment purchases
- Exclusivity or non-compete conflicting with a full-time role

## Search Interference — 1-10

- 10: Async, no minimums, droppable anytime
- 8-9: Flexible windows, rare deadlines
- 6-7: Some fixed commitments (minimum hours, reviews)
- 4-5: Fixed shifts or heavy weekly minimum
- 1-3: Blocks interviews or application hours

## Hourly Rate (AUD equiv, after platform fees) — 1-10

- 10: 90+
- 8-9: 60-90
- 6-7: 50-60
- 4-5: 40-50
- 1-3: Below the gate; do not score

## Payment Reliability — 1-10

- 10: Established platform, documented payout terms
- 8-9: Reputable platform, standard terms
- 6-7: Some reports of delays or slow support
- 4-5: Mixed reports, payout friction (PayPal-only, high minimum)
- 1-2: No verifiable payment history. Strong negative, not auto-reject; verify harder

## Skill Relevance — 1-10

- 10: Exercises target-role skills (stats, Python, ML evaluation, test automation)
- 8-9: Clearly related analytical or automation work
- 6-7: Partially related
- 4-5: Generic knowledge work
- 1-3: Unrelated (data entry, surveys)

## Income Stability — 1-10

- 10: Predictable weekly volume at the stated rate
- 8-9: Usually available, some variance
- 6-7: Availability fluctuates weekly
- 4-5: Sporadic; may vanish without notice
- 1-3: No availability expectation

## Start Friction (unpaid onboarding) — 1-10

- 10: ≤1 hr, sign-up only
- 8-9: 1-2 hrs assessment
- 6-7: 2-4 hrs
- 4-5: 4-8 hrs
- 1-3: >8 hrs or multi-stage unpaid vetting

## Overall — 1-10

- Simple average of the 6 categories: (avg/10)-(n unknowns), e.g. (6/10)-(2?)
- Modifier (gigs): Overall never exceeds Search Interference (heaviest category)
- Promote: checkpoint-decided, no fixed bar. Gig = sign up + assessment attempt (1-2 hrs, reversible); letter, plan, and resume steps do not run (no human reads them). Salaried part-time = normal workflow steps.

## Salaried part-time

- Add Location, Job Type, Seniority from [[notes/2026-applications/scoring-rubric-full-time]] to the average (9 total); Payment Reliability = employer stability; no $40 gate.

## Red flags

- Header and body rates disagree
- Per-task pay with no floor, or a high/unstated minimum payout
- Mass account-ban or withheld-pay reports
